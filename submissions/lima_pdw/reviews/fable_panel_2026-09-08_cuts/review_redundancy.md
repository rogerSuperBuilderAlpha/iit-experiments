## 1. Overall verdict

Roughly 7 percent of the body and 13 percent of the appendices is restatement the argument does
not need. The remaining length is argument, and most of it is tight: the eight hearings each say
one thing per paragraph, the derivation at 165 is performed once, and the site paragraph at 215
discloses rather than repeats. The redundancy that remains has four shapes, in descending order of
word cost:

1. **Body-to-appendix duplication.** Appendix A re-narrates lines 31–35 with the same citations in
   the same order; Appendix B repeats the Coghlan-and-Brannick separation, the researcher-blindness
   procedure, the gate's three properties, and the escalation-rights caveat from lines 213–221 nearly
   verbatim. About 500 words.
2. **The gap sentence, recycled.** "No construct models the individual capacity to coordinate
   interdependent joint work with a human counterpart through an intermediary that evaluates and
   binds both parties" appears in the abstract (9), the Introduction (19, 27), the gap section (35,
   twice), the synthesis (139, 145), and the close of the constructs section (147). Three are needed
   — abstract, Introduction, synthesis. The other four are the "same gap sentence across sections"
   pattern the house style names.
3. **Preview-then-recap pairs where the recap repeats the preview word for word.** The clearest is
   the HMC trajectory: line 61 and line 89 both enumerate the four generations and both end on
   "nearer to more humans and nearer to more consequence." The Figure 1 note (177) repeats the
   paragraph above it (175) down to the phrase "inventory of … traits." The "operational engine
   macro-structural models presuppose" sentence appears at 189 and again at 227.
4. **Self-narration.** "I perform that derivation … rather than asserting it" (113), "I state the
   claim at that width" (141), "I applied them in every hearing above" (145), "The subsections below
   do that work in order" (151), "Naming those covariates is what makes the … inheritance claims cash
   out as design commitments rather than concessions" (C566). Each tells the reader the paper was
   careful instead of being careful. The repo's style rule 4 bans exactly this; the venue exemplars
   contain zero instances.

Hedge-stacking is rare. The one habit worth naming is the doubled qualifier inside a single
sentence — "formal training cannot substitute for immersion, and … classroom instruction … cannot
alone cultivate the capacity" (185); "adjacent to … rather than through … alongside the platform
rather than under its constraints" (135); "an instrument of disintermediation, extracting the
collaboration from the system and restoring an unmediated bilateral channel" (135) — three
phrasings of one idea, where the second and third add nothing the first did not.

Recoverable with no loss of claim, evidence, or nuance: **about 735 words from the body and 500
from the appendices (Tier A)**, with a further **~330 (Tier B)** available at the author's
discretion. Body would land near 9,480 (Tier A) or 9,320 (A + B). Against the ~550–650 words of
restoration the 2 September panel priced, the Tier A cuts fund that restoration and still leave the
body a little under the 10,000 target.

## 4. What I left alone, and why

Passages that looked like repetition on a first pass and are not, or are repetition doing work:

- **The synthesis paragraph at 139.** It restates the abstract's "models centering the algorithmic
  system bracket the human counterpart, while models centering interpersonal interaction treat the
  artifact as a channel" and walks Table 1's last column in prose. After eight hearings the reader
  needs the two failure modes side by side once, and this is the place. Section-level restatement of
  a conclusion is what the house style's "say it once" rule permits.
- **The gap sentence at 9 and 19.** The abstract and the Introduction each need one; the cuts above
  remove the other four copies, not these.
- **The four conditions at 145.** Stated once in full, invoked by number three times before that.
  The problem is placement (non-specialist), not redundancy; only the glosses are in Tier B.
- **"Opaque, adaptive, (mutually) binding" — roughly twenty appearances.** This is the thread entity.
  The house style's mechanism 3 says rename it three or four ways across a stretch, and the paper does
  ("the triad," "the arrangement," "the gate," "the three properties"); the base phrase recurring is
  what holds the thread. Substituting synonyms would cost cohesion.
- **"Formally equivalent" — five appearances (25, 185, 199, 207, C566).** Each is a different speech
  act: the stake, the property, the proposition, the dissertation map, the study. It is the
  construct's own key phrase and should recur.
- **The pilot at 173 and B524.** Told in full where the argument uses it (173, as the disconfirming
  case for *specifying intent*) and where the record lives (B524). Only the third telling at 221 is
  compressed (A15). The previous panel praised "told as a rival outcome in three places with an
  honest N"; I am reducing that to two full tellings and a back-reference, not to one.
- **The derivation at 165 and the three bullets at 167–169.** ZONE. Also: the bullets' examples do
  not duplicate the opening vignette; they are the vignette's three clauses ("reading the verdict…,
  writing an explanation…, noticing when the hidden rule has moved") returned to the operations they
  name. That is the payoff of opening with the vignette, not a repeat of it.
