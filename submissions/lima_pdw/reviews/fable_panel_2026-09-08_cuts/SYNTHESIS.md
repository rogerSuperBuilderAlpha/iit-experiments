did, rounded to the nearest word the file supports.

the counterpart "unseen" at lines 9, 97, 105, 121, 153, 155, 165, 167, 168, 171, 173 while lines 15

restoration (its item 12 table) that the author has also not yet applied; section 3 nets every
figure against that debt.

The five verdicts converge on one shape. The paper's remaining slack is restatement, self-narration
and appendix over-justification, not argument; the eight hearings, the derivation, the site
paragraph and the pilot disclosures are at floor. Four of five reviewers (R, H, F, P) price the body
at roughly 10,000–10,200 after both the cuts and the restoration, and only S sees a route below
9,500 — by compressing evidence detail inside the hearings, which H's floor and F's protect list
largely block. The honest headline is that the paper will not get materially shorter than it is now
without trading differentiation for length. It will get tighter.
