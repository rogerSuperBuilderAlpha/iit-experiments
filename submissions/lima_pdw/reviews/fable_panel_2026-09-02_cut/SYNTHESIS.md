## Cross-cutting findings, ranked by convergence and severity

### 1. The counterpart at the site is visible and replies; the construct says she is "unseen" and returns nothing readable. — hostile (fatal if unanswered), comm studies (medium-high). Pre-existing; the cut exposed it.

Lines 9, 97, 105, 167, 173 carry the novelty on an *unseen* counterpart who returns "no
acknowledgment the actor can read, no next turn addressed to her, and no route to repair." Line 15
(the opening scene) and Appendix B line 479 say peer reviews and votes are public prose the student
can watch land. Both reviewers reach the same repair: what the site withholds is not the counterpart's
*meaning* but the *weight* her judgment carries in the verdict — opacity of consequence, not of
visibility. The Spitzberg paragraph already states this correctly two sentences before the grounding
argument contradicts it ("severs the link between the counterpart's evaluation and its consequence").
Comm studies adds that the Walther hearing already runs on who *evaluates and replies*, so it survives
the correction untouched. Hostile adds the one-paragraph obligation that follows: say why opacity of
consequence is not CMC competence plus Rahman's reactivity — the answer is that the *same act* is read
by both parties, which the pilot's machine-only routing illustrates.

**Fix (~60 words changed, none added):** rewrite the two grounding sentences at 97 so the withheld
thing is the evidence-to-consequence link; remove or make true "unseen" at 9, 121, 167, 168; derive the
*interpreting*/*specifying intent* split at 171 from the *evaluator's* silence, not the counterpart's.

### 2. A compression artifact at line 171 narrows *interpreting* to the machine. — comm studies (Part 2), hostile (Attack 2). Created by the cut.

The pre-cut sentence read "inbound and outbound understanding"; the cut changed it to "machine-facing
decoding and counterpart-facing encoding." The definition at 167 has *interpreting* reconstruct both
the intermediary and the counterpart. Hostile reads the same passage as a derivation that covers only
the machine-facing half of each operation, with every counterpart clause riding on channel closure
and joint work — which appear in the abstract, Introduction, synthesis and Conclusion but not in the
scope conditions (155) or the derivation (165). **Fix:** revert 171 to "inbound and outbound";
consider hostile's fourth scope condition (interdependent work, intermediary as the only channel of
consequence) stated at 155 and used at 165, which also answers item 1.

### 3. Definition, scope conditions and derivation do not name the same three properties. — org theory (high), hostile (Attack 2). Pre-existing; unflagged last round.

Line 21's definition says "three terms carry the structure: opacity, adaptivity, intermediary."
Lines 153, 155, 165 run on opacity, adaptivity, *bindingness*. Bindingness is the differentiator in
Table 1, the four conditions and the site paragraph, and it is absent from the sentence that defines
the construct. **Fix (one sentence):** put "that evaluates and binds both parties" in the definition
and name bindingness as the third term, glossing "intermediary" as the seat the three properties
attach to.

### 4. The measurement model is stated three ways, and the cut removed the reconciliation. — methodology (major), hostile (Attack 11), non-specialist (#28). Partly created by the cut.

Line 175: "neither reflective indicators of a single latent trait nor formative components." Line
225: do they "load onto a coherent higher-order factor"? Appendix C 556–562: a CFA with per-operation
latent correlations, which is three first-order reflective factors by construction. The pre-cut
paragraph divided the labor (separable for discriminant testing; the loop is a process claim tested
qualitatively); the cut kept the negation and dropped the division. Methodology adds that the loop
has no disconfirming criterion anywhere — Table B1 is per-operation. **Fix:** replace the 175
sentence with the model (three correlated first-order factors; higher-order structure tested by
model comparison in Study 2); add a Table B1 row for the recursive-process claim keyed to episode
order; delete "the survey establishes" for a panel the paper says cannot establish it.

### 5. Who retunes the rule, and the site lacks the form's terminating sanction. — methodology (major + moderate), hostile (severe), org theory (Part 2 item 5), non-specialist (#34). Retune: pre-existing. Termination sentence: created by the cut.

Line 213 pledges no further interventions; line 215 says weights and thresholds "are retuned over the
term" in the passive. If the designer retunes mid-term the pledge fails and adaptivity is an
experimenter manipulation; if a pre-committed schedule retunes, the paper must say so. Appendix C
554's "freezing the gate configuration" for pooling would remove the adaptivity the scope conditions
require — freeze the *retuning schedule*, not the rule. Methodology also names the unclaimed asset:
the designer holds the true rule, so *interpreting* can be scored for accuracy against it, which no
commercial-platform study can do. Separately, four reviewers want the deleted sentence back — "A
student's option to drop a course is not the analogue of unilateral account termination" — because
without it the exit-rights caveat reads as clerical rather than structural, and the form's anchoring
sanction is absent at the site. Methodology adds the stakes gradient (grade-level, not
livelihood-level; Rahman's constrained reactivity may not surface). **Fix:** one disclosure paragraph
in Appendix B (who/what retunes, on what schedule, fixed when, known to whom); reword 554; claim the
accuracy criterion; restore the termination sentence (18 words) plus one sentence on stakes.

### 6. RQ4's comparison partner regressed to *keeping track* in the body. — org theory (moderate), methodology (moderate). Created by the cut.

Lines 171, B456, C548 and Table B1 house attribution in *interpreting* and test separation from it
first. The body's RQ4 sentence at 219 now supplies only the tracking contrast; the sentence naming the
primary comparison was cut. (Hostile read 219 as consistent; the two reviewers who checked the pre-cut
text did not.) **Fix (~20 words):** "RQ4 tests whether attribution — housed provisionally within
*interpreting* — separates from that operation and, secondarily, from *keeping track*."

### 7. Manky's Lima drivers are outside the triad at line 19 and inside its core at 147 and 155. — org theory (high), hostile (Attack 12), comm studies (note), non-specialist (#7, #11). Pre-existing; the narrowing made it bite.

Line 19 says of all four fragments that "in none of them does that system return a single
determination binding both sides." Ride-hailing binds driver and passenger; line 155 places dispatch
networks at the construct's core. Hostile adds that passenger screening is not "interdependent joint
work," so Manky now sits outside the paper's own domain either way. Comm studies flags the Manky
mechanism as the paper's most load-bearing empirical citation the card could not verify against the
article body. Non-specialist wants the Lima stake sentence back in the Introduction (cut at 25) and
notes Lima gets two sentences at the tail of a section a tired reader skims. **Fix:** separate Manky
from the folk-theories trio at 19 ("comes closest, and differs in kind: ride-hailing does bind both
parties; what his study lacks is the construct, not the configuration"); restore the driver sentence at
25 (~25 words); verify Manky against the article before Lima.

### 8. The Zhou et al. antecedents sentence says what the cards cannot support. — comm studies (medium), hostile (Attack 9a). Pre-existing; survived the cut unchanged.

Line 121: "the primary antecedents … are social — informal peer support moderated by collectivist
orientation." The project's verified re-read of Zhou's Table 6 records peer support *b* = .23 and
cognitive job crafting *b* = .41 — the larger antecedent is an individual cognitive mechanism by the
authors' own definition — and neither Zhou card records a collectivist-orientation moderator. This is
the one sentence in the eight hearings that attributes to a source what the cards do not confirm.
**Fix:** "Peer social support is an antecedent in their structural model, alongside cognitive job
crafting"; drop collectivism unless the page can be produced.

### 9. DeVito (2021) is the uncited rival that holds three of the four properties. — hostile (serious), comm studies (F13). Pre-existing.

The folk-theories rebuttal disposes of Bucher 2017, DeVito et al. 2018 and Litt 2012. DeVito's 2021
"Adaptive folk theorization" adds change detection (*keeping track* named and evidenced), a
four-level complexity classification of equal-standing users (uneven distribution observed),
acquisition through participation, and explicit teaching of the literacy (the published case against
line 185's instruction claim). The project's own card calls it "the most dangerous single source in
this sub-cluster." Comm studies adds that the interpret-in-order-to-present *pairing* the recursive
paragraph presents as the construct's property is DeVito's mechanism and should be conceded as prior
art so that the derivation, not the pairing, is what the paper claims. **Fix:** one paragraph at 141
or 185 (~120 words): concede the pairing and the adaptation; hold the counterpart, the binding and the
joint work.

### 10. The propositions and the Paradox cross the capacity/standing line, and the aggregation mechanism is asserted. — hostile (Attacks 6–7, serious), org theory (F3 moderate, F5 moderate), methodology (F11 minor), non-specialist (#32). Mostly pre-existing; P3's continuum footing created by the cut.

Three overlapping points. (a) Line 153 draws the boundary between capacity and standing; P1
(procedural justice), P2 (inequality) and P3 (institutional lever) each predicate standing outcomes.
Hostile: say the construct's *content* stops at capacity and its *implications* reach standing. (b)
P3 has lost its footing: the pre-cut scope continuum ("arrangements fall along a continuum according
to how strictly the three properties govern") was cut, so 155 now reads as a switch while P3 treats
opacity as a dial; hostile adds that "the level of algorithmacy *required*" is not a measurable
quantity — the panel measures algorithmacy *held*. (c) The aggregation mechanism at 193 is additive by
default; org theory shows that under the paper's own socialization property a cohort's mean
algorithmacy and its shared working theory of the gate are one quantity measured twice, so Study 3
cannot separate them; hostile shows that the same sentence performs the crumple-zone move the
paragraph has just named, and asks for a design commitment (every capacity estimate reported beside an
arrangement estimate) rather than a distinction. Methodology adds that P1 is assigned to no study and
its dependent variable is measured nowhere. **Fix:** rewrite 153's boundary sentence; restore the
continuum sentence at 155 (~20 words); drop "required" from P3; one paragraph at 193 or Study 3
stating the aggregation function is not assumed additive and naming cohort-level shared theory as a
separate variable; assign P1 to Study 3 with appeals-granted and disclosures-won as outcomes.

### 11. The nomological network claims four testable, "pre-registered" relations and Appendix C instruments one. — methodology (major), hostile (Attack 9b, 10), org theory (smaller point). Pre-existing; the cut removed two rationales.

Rahman and Sutherland built typologies, not scales, so those two predictions have no test anywhere;
Spitzberg's measure exists and is not in Study 2; nothing is registered. The *Embracing* null called
"the sharpest single test" is what any skill-like measure produces against an attitude scale (Zhou
already found *r* = .04 with digital competence) and has no equivalence bound; the sharp test in the
paragraph is the machine-heuristic partialling — give it the label. Hostile adds that every covariate
the hearings promise (Walther's slack, Rahman's dependence and shocks, Nass and Moon, Sundar and Kim) is
cashed in Study 3, whose "sample and setting remain to be specified." **Fix:** claim in the body only
what Study 2 tests; "to be pre-registered before Study 2 fielding"; add Spitzberg's measure and an
equivalence bound; move the short covariate scales into the Paper 3 panel or stop presenting them as
answered; restore the Rahman and Spitzberg rationales at 159 (~50 words).

## Single-lens findings worth acting on

- **The three-wave panel has no algorithmacy instrument** (methodology F1, major). Items are built in
  Study 2 from the Study 1 corpus, so wave 2's "convergence check" and the "developmental
  trajectories" measure nothing yet stated. Name provisional items, or the behavioral indicators the
  gate and repository already compute (reviewer accuracy, resubmission counts, description revisions,
  time-to-clear), or both.
- **Table B1 cannot tell construct failure from gate misspecification** (methodology F5). Both
  disconfirming patterns for *interpreting* and *specifying intent* also follow from a gate whose peer
  weight is negligible; the author knows the weights and can add the threshold. State whether the
  pilot participant's submission passed.
- **A ninth candidate the paper never hears** (hostile Attack 3): algorithmically weighted peer
  assessment and student feedback literacy — Calibrated Peer Review, Piech et al. 2013 on tuned
  peer-grader reliability, Carless and Boud 2018. Flagged by the reviewer as from memory and
  **unverified**; if it holds, it both threatens the "no construct" claim's field coverage and helps the
  author, because the site becomes an instance of a known class rather than a one-off he built.
  Verify before citing.
- **Simmel's arbitrator vs. *tertius*** (hostile Attack 5, serious). Hostile argues that an arbitrator
  stripped of conferral is Simmel's *tertius gaudens* / Burt's broker, that the cut removed the
  Johnson/Burt paragraph that had the right label and the only account of *why* the intermediary
  withholds, and asks for ~90 words back. **Contested:** org theory calls the arbitrator-without-conferral
  sentence "the sharpest single sentence about what a platform is" and both org theory and comm
  studies list Johnson/Burt as rightly gone because Simmel's *tertius* at 41 already carries "the asset
  is the disconnection." Author's call; the cheap middle is one clause at 151 naming the platform as
  *tertius* by structure and arbitrator by self-description.
- **HMC trajectory presented as chronology** (comm F1). Lines 61 and 75 say "over three decades" and
  "next generation"; Jung 2015 predates Guzman 2018 and AI-MC is contemporary with the communicative
  subject. Line 43 already states the right principle (ordered by standing). One clause each.
- **"Only two of the seventeen reference human actors"** (comm F8) is contestable against Long and
  Magerko's table; use the steelman's positional form ("no competency names another person as a party
  to the interaction").
- **Litt 2012 credited with an algorithm her article lacks** (comm F11) at 19 and 141; Litt names the
  anticipatory move with no third party in the picture.
- **Spitzberg 2015 "impression withheld" at 181 vs. "impression relocated" at 99** (comm Part 2).
- **Body lacks the analytic logic** (methodology F9): abduction, stopping rule and the pre-specified
  negative case appear zero times between lines 13 and 228; the cut plan promised a parenthetical and
  the execution removed the paragraph.
- **Appendix B claims elicitation across three concealment regimes with no corpus** (methodology F7);
  the strategic-site warrant is argued for the Trinidad cohorts, which yield no Paper 3 data.
- **Ethics disclosures** (methodology F10): "consent" appears nowhere; transcript custody, opt-in vs.
  required, and the interviewer agent's fixed prompt version are unstated.
- **The Conclusion never returns to the student, the inequality or Lima** (non-spec #35); ~60 words.
- **Ten bare terms for a lay reader** (non-spec): micro-foundation, pull request, structurational,
  methodological individualism, molar/molecular, nomological network, SNAIL, covariates, Hult, critical
  incident. The four analytical conditions are invoked by number at 43, 61 and 103 and stated at 145.
